TroveGuard

Automatic top-ups and repayments for your MUSD trove on Mezo Testnet. Contract 0xa67c9bbcd4350b24e33e25e2ebd7c80c04409df7 · Radar

Your trove

Enter a borrower address or connect a wallet.

1 · Arm the guard

The guard fires when ICR is strictly below the trigger. It must be between 110 % (MCR) and 1000 %. The tip is paid in BTC from the reserve to whoever calls protect.

2 · Sign a permit ladder

Your wallet signs one EIP-712 message per rung (AddColl or RepayMUSD for BorrowerOperationsSignatures) for consecutive nonces from your current one. Signatures stay in your wallet until you send them; this page never sees a private key. A repayment must leave net debt at or above 1,800 MUSD.

3 · Withdraw or cancel

Transactions

    Testnet only, not audited. Every transaction is simulated first and then signed in your wallet.
