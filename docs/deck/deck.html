The problem

Borrowers can lose their BTC three ways, and nobody shows them where they stand

Liquidation

Below 110 % collateral ratio the trove is closed and the BTC goes to the Stability Pool.

Recovery Mode

If the system ratio falls below 150 %, troves under 150 % can be liquidated too.

Redemptions

Anyone can swap MUSD for BTC. It is taken from the lowest-ratio troves first.

  • The Mezo app shows your own trove. It does not show your place among the other 326, or how close the system is to Recovery Mode.
  • A warning only helps if you are awake to act on it. Keepers and Stability Pool depositors have no simple view of what is liquidatable and whether it pays.

The solution

One tool that reads the MUSD contracts directly, pinned to one block

  • Radar and history: every trove's ratio, liquidation price, a BTC price-shock stress test, Stability Pool coverage, trends, and a 3-day ICR chart per trove.
  • Redemption queue: your position, the MUSD ahead of you, and what a redemption of X MUSD would hit.
  • Alerts: a browser watchlist with notifications, Slack, Discord, Telegram or a signed webhook, and a Cloudflare Worker with a Telegram bot and an Atom feed per trove.
  • TroveGuard v3: a ladder of pre-signed adjust, top-up and repay steps that any keeper runs below your trigger, up to a target ratio in one call.
  • Keeper daemon: liquidations and multi-step guard protect calls, simulated first and sent only when they pay.

TroveGuard v3

One keeper call lifts a trove to your target ratio

1

Arm

Set a trigger, a target ratio, max steps per call and a tip. Park BTC and/or MUSD as a reserve.

2

Sign a ladder

One EIP-712 permit per step: Adjust (BTC in and debt down at once), AddColl or RepayMUSD.

3

Protect

Below the trigger, or near the redemption front, anyone calls protect(). Steps run until the target is reached; funds only enter your own trove.

4

Cancel

cancel refunds every reserve and invalidates every stored permit.

The web flow and the keeper

A wallet page for borrowers, a daemon for keepers

guard.html

  • Any EIP-1193 wallet: switch to Mezo Testnet, see your trove and guard, arm it, fund it, and build the ladder step by step with the expected ratio after each one and a net-debt check, stored in one storePermits tx.
  • Every transaction is simulated before the wallet prompt. No keys, nothing loaded from other origins.

Keeper daemon

  • keeper --loop --min-profit 5 --send: eth_call before every send, a tip per step, hints recomputed for the trove after all steps, backoff, JSON logs, Docker image.
  • On testnet it liquidated a 3,873 MUSD trove, skipped three below its minimum profit, and recovered from an RPC error by itself.
