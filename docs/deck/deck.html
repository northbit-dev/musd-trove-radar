The problem

Borrowers can lose their BTC three ways, and nobody shows them where they stand

Liquidation

Below 110 % collateral ratio the trove is closed and the BTC goes to the Stability Pool.

Recovery Mode

If the system ratio falls below 150 %, troves under 150 % can be liquidated too.

Redemptions

Anyone can swap MUSD for BTC. It is taken from the lowest-ratio troves first.

  • The Mezo app shows your own trove. It does not show your place among the other 352, or how close the system is to Recovery Mode.
  • A warning only helps if you are awake to act on it. Keepers and Stability Pool depositors have no simple view of what is liquidatable and whether it pays.

The solution

One tool that reads the MUSD contracts directly, pinned to one block

  • Radar and history: every trove's ratio, liquidation price, a BTC price-shock stress test, Stability Pool coverage, and trends over time.
  • Redemption queue: your position, the MUSD ahead of you, and what a redemption of X MUSD would hit.
  • Alerts: Slack, Discord, Telegram or a signed webhook, on thresholds and when a trove is redeemed or liquidated.
  • TroveGuard: pre-signed top-ups or repayments that any keeper can execute below your trigger.
  • Keeper daemon: liquidations and guard protect calls, simulated first and sent only when they pay.

TroveGuard v2

Top up or repay automatically, while you sleep

1

Arm

Set a trigger ratio and a keeper tip. Park BTC and/or MUSD in the guard as a reserve.

2

Sign a ladder

One EIP-712 permit per rung, for consecutive nonces: AddColl (BTC in) or RepayMUSD (debt down).

3

Protect

Below the trigger, anyone calls protect(). One rung is spent, funds only enter your own trove, the caller earns the tip.

4

Cancel

cancel refunds every reserve and invalidates every stored permit.

The web flow and the keeper

A wallet page for borrowers, a daemon for keepers

guard.html

  • Any EIP-1193 wallet: switch to Mezo Testnet, see your trove and guard, arm it, fund it, sign the ladder in one storePermits tx.
  • Every transaction is simulated before the wallet prompt. No keys, nothing loaded from other origins.

Keeper daemon

  • keeper --loop --min-profit 5 --send: eth_call before every send, backoff, JSON logs, Docker image.
  • On testnet it liquidated a 3,873 MUSD trove, skipped three below its minimum profit, and recovered from an RPC error by itself.
